    Power and Dimming¶

    The light ECU (can_sim) has a dimmable lamp output with an electrical model, so current, voltage, a fuse and electrical faults behave like on a real control unit. The NUCLEO gateway dims its LED with PWM accordingly.

    supply 12.6 V, Ri 50 mOhm -- fuse 3 A slow -- smart high-side switch (PWM) -- 20 mOhm -- 21 W bulb -- GND

    Dimming¶

    Source How
    Visualizer mouse wheel: DIMMER_CMD in 10 % steps
    Any node, scenario send 201#32 (DIMMER_CMD, data[0] = 0..100 %)
    can_sim console / test bench dim 50
    UDS uds_client session extended + write 0105 32

    The ECU sends the brightness in LAMP_CMD byte 1 (LampLevel). The visualizer's lamp and the gateway's LED (TIM2 PWM, 1 kHz) follow it. Senders that only send byte 0 still work: receivers treat a missing byte as 100 %.

    Electrical model (libs/power)¶

    PowerPath is a quasi-static model that is advanced every control step:

    • Supply: open-circuit voltage and internal resistance; the terminal voltage drops with the current.
    • Fuse (3 A, slow blow): I²t model. While the RMS current is above the rating, the excess (I² - In²) * t heats it up. It blows at 40 A²s and cools down at 5 A²s/s below the rating.
    • Smart high-side switch: PWM, current sense, shutdown above 15 A and a retry every 100 ms (thermal restart). Switching the output off resets it.
    • Load: resistive, R = U² / P (21 W at 12 V gives 6.86 Ω, about 1.82 A at 12.6 V).

    With PWM the average current is duty * I_on, and the fuse sees the RMS value sqrt(duty) * I_on.

    Faults and what the ECU sees¶

    Faults change the circuit. The ECU's monitors only use measured values (current sense, supply voltage), like real software:

    Fault (fault ... on) Circuit Reaction DTC
    short load resistance 0 switch shuts down, retries every 100 ms B1001-11
    open no load no current although on B1001-13
    overload bulb with 3x power (63 W, 5.3 A) overcurrent after 200 ms; fuse blows after about 2 s, lamp dark B1001-19, then B1003-13
    supply 9.8 V source supply below 10.5 V B1002-16

    A blown fuse stays blown until fuse replace (console or bench ecu fuse replace). Removing the overload alone does not bring the lamp back.

    LAMP_STATUS (0x101)¶

    Sent by the ECU every 200 ms, Intel byte order (see data/dbc/iotwin.dbc):

    Bytes Signal Unit
    0-1 LampCurrent mA (factor 0.001 A)
    2-3 SupplyVoltage mV (factor 0.001 V)
    4 OutputDuty %
    5 bit 0 FuseBlown
    5 bit 1 Overcurrent (DTC test failed)
    5 bit 2 ShortShutdown

    The visualizer HUD shows current and voltage from it, the schematic view decodes it at the ECU node.

    Diagnostics¶

    DID Content
    0104 measured supply voltage, mV
    0105 brightness 0..100 % (read/write, extended session)
    0106 lamp current, mA
    0107 fuse: blown (0/1), heat in % of the melting integral
    0204 (gateway) LED brightness: commanded %, measured %

    The gateway measures its own PWM: it samples the LED pin's input register for about 5 ms. This checks the real pin and not just the timer registers. Verified on the NUCLEO: 100/50/25/10/0 % commanded gave the same values measured.
