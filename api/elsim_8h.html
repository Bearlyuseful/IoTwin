Detailed Description

Electrical installation of a house: model, time steps, protection, measurements.

See docs/adr/0007-electrical-simulation-core.md and docs/dev/electrical.md.

  • A device has typed terminals, referred to as "device:terminal" (e.g. "lamp_living:L"). Every terminal is a node of the network.
  • A connection is one conductor between two terminals (role L, N or PE); its resistance comes from its cable (copper, length, cross-section) or is a short internal link.
  • el_model_step() solves the network (complex phasors, 50 Hz), evaluates breakers, fuses and RCDs, re-solves after a trip and records events.
  • Measurements see the same network: voltage and Thevenin impedance between two terminals (loop impedance, continuity, insulation).

Terminals per type: supply L1 L2 L3 N PE breaker, fuse, switch in out rcd L_in N_in L_out N_out load L N PE socket L N PE
