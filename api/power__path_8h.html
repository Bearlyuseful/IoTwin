Detailed Description

Electrical model of one switched output of an ECU (quasi-static, per time step).

supply (U0, Ri) – fuse – smart high-side switch (PWM) – wire – resistive load – GND

  • Supply: open circuit voltage and internal resistance; the terminal voltage drops with the load current.
  • Fuse: slow blow, I2t model. While the RMS current is above the rating the excess (I^2 - In^2) * t heats it up; it blows when the melting integral is reached and cools down otherwise.
  • Smart switch (e.g. PROFET type): PWM with duty 0..1, current limit with shutdown, automatic restart after restart_ms (thermal retry), current sense.
  • Load: resistive, R = Un^2 / Pn; faults: short (R = 0), open (no current), overload factor (power factor > 1 = lower resistance, e.g. wrong bulb).

Currents are averaged over the PWM period, the fuse uses the RMS value. Portable C without I/O, unit tested on the host.
