Detailed Description

Output stage, electrical model and UDS diagnostics of the simulated light ECU (see docs/dev/diagnostics.md and docs/dev/power.md).

The lamp output is a PowerPath: 12.6 V supply -> 3 A slow fuse -> smart high-side switch with PWM (brightness) and current sense -> 21 W bulb. Injected faults change that circuit, monitors turn the measured values into DTCs the way a real ECU would:

  • short to ground: the smart switch shuts down (restart every 100 ms) B1001-11
  • open load: no current while the output is on B1001-13
  • overload (three times the power): current above 1.5 x nominal B1001-19, the fuse blows after about two seconds B1003-13
  • supply voltage below 10.5 V B1002-16

UDS: requests on 0x7E0 (and functional 0x7DF), responses on 0x7E8. LAMP_STATUS (0x101) reports current, supply voltage, duty and flags every 200 ms.

Free of I/O and threads, unit tested on the host.
