Detailed Description

Runs the electrical simulation core (libs/elsim) for the scene:

  • builds the model from the scene (ports become junctions, IElectricalElement actors and components add devices and cables) and rebuilds it when the wiring changes;
  • steps it in its own thread with a fixed time step (default 10 ms), independent of the frame rate;
  • takes the newest snapshot and the events every frame and hands them to the elements; commands (switch, dim, faults, reset) go the other way.

Unreal owns the topology, the simulator every electrical value. -IoTwinElectricalDisabled switches it off, -IoTwinShortCircuitAfter= puts a short circuit on the first load after s seconds (smoke tests).
