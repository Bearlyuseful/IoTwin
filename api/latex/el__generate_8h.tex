\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Synthetic installations of any size, for benchmarks and tests. 

A house with a three-\/phase supply (TN-\/S), RCD groups spread over the three phases, final circuits with breakers (lighting B10 with 1.\+5 mm\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}2, sockets B16 with 2.\+5 mm\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}2) and a chain of junction boxes per circuit. Lighting boxes feed a switch and a lamp, socket boxes a socket with an appliance plugged in now and then. The same seed gives the same house.
