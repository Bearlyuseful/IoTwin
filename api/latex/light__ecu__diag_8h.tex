\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Output stage, electrical model and UDS diagnostics of the simulated light ECU (see docs/dev/diagnostics.\+md and docs/dev/power.\+md). 

The lamp output is a \doxylink{structPowerPath}{Power\+Path}\+: 12.\+6 V supply -\/\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>} 3 A slow fuse -\/\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>} smart high-\/side switch with PWM (brightness) and current sense -\/\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>} 21 W bulb. Injected faults change that circuit, monitors turn the measured values into DTCs the way a real ECU would\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item short to ground\+: the smart switch shuts down (restart every 100 ms) B1001-\/11
\item open load\+: no current while the output is on B1001-\/13
\item overload (three times the power)\+: current above 1.\+5 x nominal B1001-\/19, the fuse blows after about two seconds B1003-\/13
\item supply voltage below 10.\+5 V B1002-\/16
\end{DoxyItemize}

UDS\+: requests on 0x7\+E0 (and functional 0x7\+DF), responses on 0x7\+E8. LAMP\+\_\+\+STATUS (0x101) reports current, supply voltage, duty and flags every 200 ms.

Free of I/O and threads, unit tested on the host.
