\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Electrical model of one switched output of an ECU (quasi-\/static, per time step). 

supply (U0, Ri) -- fuse -- smart high-\/side switch (PWM) -- wire -- resistive load -- GND


\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Supply\+: open circuit voltage and internal resistance; the terminal voltage drops with the load current.
\item \doxylink{structFuse}{Fuse}\+: slow blow, I2t model. While the RMS current is above the rating the excess (I\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}2 -\/ In\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}2) \texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*} t heats it up; it blows when the melting integral is reached and cools down otherwise.
\item Smart switch (e.\+g. PROFET type)\+: PWM with duty 0..1, current limit with shutdown, automatic restart after restart\+\_\+ms (thermal retry), current sense.
\item Load\+: resistive, R = Un\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}2 / Pn; faults\+: short (R = 0), open (no current), overload factor (power factor \texorpdfstring{$>$}{>} 1 = lower resistance, e.\+g. wrong bulb).
\end{DoxyItemize}

Currents are averaged over the PWM period, the fuse uses the RMS value. Portable C without I/O, unit tested on the host.
