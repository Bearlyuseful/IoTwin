\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Electrical installation of a house\+: model, time steps, protection, measurements. 

See docs/adr/0007-\/electrical-\/simulation-\/core.\+md and docs/dev/electrical.\+md.


\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item A {\bfseries{device}} has typed {\bfseries{terminals}}, referred to as "{}device\+:terminal"{} (e.\+g. "{}lamp\+\_\+living\+:\+L"{}). Every terminal is a node of the network.
\item A {\bfseries{connection}} is one conductor between two terminals (role L, N or PE); its resistance comes from its {\bfseries{cable}} (copper, length, cross-\/section) or is a short internal link.
\item \doxylink{elsim_8h_aab5e45876d1ab22015c30e1e4609ffe1}{el\+\_\+model\+\_\+step()} solves the network (complex phasors, 50 Hz), evaluates breakers, fuses and RCDs, re-\/solves after a trip and records events.
\item Measurements see the same network\+: voltage and Thevenin impedance between two terminals (loop impedance, continuity, insulation).
\end{DoxyItemize}

Terminals per type\+: supply L1 L2 L3 N PE breaker, fuse, switch in out rcd L\+\_\+in N\+\_\+in L\+\_\+out N\+\_\+out load L N PE socket L N PE
